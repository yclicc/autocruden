Pancakes and Dates Reading Plan

Inspired by the fact that in 2026 both Ramadan and Lent begin on the same day (or nearly), this page presents a 30 day reading plan for the entire Qur'an (in the Yusuf Ali English translation) as well as the traditional 30 day reading plan for the Psalms (known to Muslims as Zabur and to Jews as Tehillim) from the English Book of Common Prayer. Restricting to the Psalms is a practical decision taken because the Bible entire would be very hard to read in 30 days, and the Psalms are perhaps the most beautiful and poetic section of the Bible. As there doesn't seem to be a standardised reading plan for the Qur'an, this one has been created using dynamic programming to give the optimal even division across 60 sessions whereby no Surah is split up and they are all read in the normal order.
The translation of the Bible on this page is the Berean Standard Bible, a recent public domain translation. Since this is not widely used a link to the much more popular New International Version (NIV) is also supplied, as well as links to the similar Arabic NAV (New Arabic Version). Similarly, links are provided to Quran.com for those who prefer a different English translation or to read the original Arabic text. Note that the verse numbers of Yusuf Ali are not necessarily consistent with the Kufic numbering system that can be found on Wikipedia, but that the more conventional verse numbers were used in the computation of the optimal reading plan.
We don't consider it to be possible to endorse both the Qur'an and the Bible since, amongst other things, they differ on the nature of God and therefore necessarily on the way of salvation. The reading plan here is provided for both Christians and Muslims to be more familiar with both texts, their similarities and differences.

Qur'an text: Yusuf Ali English translation, adapted from github.com/islamAndAi/QURAN-NLP.

Psalms text: The Holy Bible, Berean Standard Bible, BSB is produced in cooperation with Bible Hub, Discovery Bible, OpenBible.com, and the Berean Bible Translation Committee. This text of God's Word has been dedicated to the public domain.
